Talk with the pastor and Church Office about anniversary observances, services of thanksgiving, and other special worship services. They can help you discuss the service and arrangements appropriate to your occasion.

Gather to celebrate

For a stand-alone reception or social event—such as a birthday, bridal shower, baby shower, or anniversary celebration—that does not accompany a wedding, funeral, worship service, or other special service, choose the arrangement that fits your gathering.

With a coordinator

$200

The coordinator provides the services and oversight normally associated with that role for the amount of time agreed upon in advance.

Additional coordinator fees apply if the gathering continues beyond the agreed ending time, using the schedule below.

Without a coordinator

No coordinator fee

The person or group using the facility takes responsibility for the event, including all setup and cleanup needed to leave the space in church-ready condition at the conclusion of the event.

Plan your volunteers and allow time to restore the space before you leave.

Separate facility-use fees and other church charges apply whether or not you use a coordinator. The Church Office can confirm the charges for your event.

Agree on the time together

A special service that begins at least 20 minutes late incurs an additional coordinator fee. The same schedule applies when a reception or social event with a coordinator continues beyond its agreed ending time.

Delay or extra timeAdditional coordinator fee
0–19 minutesNo fee
20–49 minutes$50
50–79 minutes$100
80–109 minutes$150
Each additional 30 minutesAnother $50

For special services subject to the late-fee policy, the organist also receives $50 when the service begins 20 minutes late, plus $50 for each additional 30 minutes of delay during which the organist must remain.

Reception late fees are separate from any fees caused by a service starting late. These fees recognize the additional time committed by coordinators and musicians.

Start with the Church Office

Contact the pastor or the Church Office to begin planning your gathering.

When you get in touch, share the kind of occasion you are planning, your preferred date and times, the space you hope to use, and whether you are considering a coordinator. For a worship service, include the pastor in the conversation.

Confirm the applicable facility and service charges, the agreed ending time, and responsibilities for setup and cleanup before finalizing your plans.
