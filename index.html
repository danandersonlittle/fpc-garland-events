A service with meaning

Begin with the pastor

A wedding in the church is a worship service with God as its focus. The pastor of First Presbyterian Church normally officiates. Another ordained minister may assist or conduct the ceremony with the pastor’s approval.

The church’s pastor has final approval of all elements of the service, following the Directory for Worship in the PC(USA) Book of Order.

Plan together

Schedule your wedding and rehearsal with the pastor and Church Office. Before the wedding, the officiating minister will meet with you to plan the ceremony and ensure adequate preparation for marriage.

Choose your music

Music should normally be sacred. The officiating minister and organist will help you choose music and have final approval. The church organist ordinarily plays; a guest organist must arrange a meeting with the church organist to review operation of the organ and other instruments.

Preparing the space

Flowers & decorations

  • Choose decorations that support worship without being unduly elaborate or ostentatious.
  • Do not use nails, screws, glue, or materials that could damage furniture, walls, floors, or ceilings.
  • Candelabra may be used in the Sanctuary and Fellowship Center with protective shields and drop cloths. Candles are not permitted at the ends of pews.
  • Chancel furniture normally remains in place; exceptions require the pastor’s approval.
  • Remove rented items, flowers used in the service, and gifts left in the narthex immediately after the service.
  • Rice and confetti may not be thrown anywhere on church grounds.

Photography & video

Photographers and videographers must speak with the officiating minister before the wedding and respect the minister’s direction and the worship nature of the service.

Flash photography is not permitted during the service.

Continue the celebration

Receptions are normally held in the Fellowship Center. Smaller receptions may be held in the Connection Center with Session approval.

The kitchen may be used to prepare refreshments, and available refrigerator space may be used. Cooking is not permitted. Leave the kitchen clean and return all items to their proper places. Additional cleaning and replacement of lost or broken items will be billed to the wedding party.

Agree on your reception’s ending time in advance. The reception coordinator fee covers that agreed period.
