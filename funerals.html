A service of remembrance and hope

Gather in worship

A funeral or memorial service is a worship service with God as its focus. All elements of a Sunday worship service are appropriate for this gathering as we remember, pray, and give thanks.

Members of First Presbyterian Church are strongly encouraged to hold their funeral or memorial service in the church. Whether you are planning ahead or making arrangements after a death, begin with a conversation with the pastor.

Plan with the pastor

The pastor of First Presbyterian Church normally officiates. Another qualified person may assist or conduct the service with the pastor’s approval.

Whether or not the church’s pastor officiates, the pastor has final approval of all elements of the service, following the Directory for Worship in the PC(USA) Book of Order.

The pastor and Church Office can help you discuss the service, music, and arrangements for gathering with family and friends.

Flowers & decorations

Simple, thoughtful flowers and decorations can help create a fitting setting for worship and remembrance.

  • Decorations and other accessories should not be unduly elaborate or ostentatious, so that they do not detract from the worship of God.
  • Do not use nails, screws, glue, or any material that could damage furniture, walls, floors, or ceilings.
  • All decorations used in the funeral or memorial service must be removed immediately after the service.

Time together after the service

The church building is available for a reception following a funeral or memorial service. Receptions are held in the Connection Center or Fellowship Center, depending on the number attending and the extent of the food to be served.

Refreshments & the kitchen

The kitchen may be used to prepare refreshments, and available refrigerator space may be used. Cooking is not permitted.

At the conclusion of the reception, leave the kitchen clean and return all used items to their proper places. Additional cleaning and replacement of lost or broken items will be billed to the family.

Help with a meal

If you ask the coordinator to arrange and serve a meal for family or others before or after the service, an additional $50 applies, plus reimbursement by the family for the actual cost of the food.

Discuss reception and meal arrangements with the Church Office so that responsibilities and any applicable charges are clear.

Making payment

You may pay the service providers directly, or write one check to First Presbyterian Church of Garland and the church will distribute the payments to the providers.

Payments are normally due on the day of the funeral or memorial service. The Church Office can help you confirm payment arrangements.
